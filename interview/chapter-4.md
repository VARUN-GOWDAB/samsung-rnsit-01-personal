# Part B -- Scenario-Based Questions (5)

### 21.

confidential financial information. Based on the chapter, what ethical
concerns should the company consider before acting?

strong qualifications. Which ethical issue discussed in the chapter does
this illustrate, and what governance practices could reduce the problem?

### 23.

customers. Which concept from the chapter does this represent, and what

understand how the recommendations are generated. Which ethical
