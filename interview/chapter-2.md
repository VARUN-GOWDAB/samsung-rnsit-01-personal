# Interview Practice Questions -- Chapter 2: Prompt Engineering Basics

## Conceptual Questions (20)

1.  What is prompt engineering, and why is it important in modern AI?
2.  Why has prompt engineering become more important in the era of
    multimodal models?
3.  How do AI agents differ from traditional AI applications?
4.  What role does automation play in prompt-centric AI systems?
5.  Why is natural language considered the new command interface for AI?
6.  How does a prompt act as a control mechanism for AI systems?
7.  What are the key differences between text-only AI and multimodal AI?
8.  Why does input quality determine output quality?
9.  What characteristics make a prompt vague?
10. What elements make a prompt clear and actionable?
11. What are the typical consequences of poorly designed prompts?
12. How do engineered prompts reduce time and token cost?
13. Compare naive prompts with engineered prompts.
14. Why are constraints important in prompt design?
15. What is the significance of specifying an output format in a prompt?
16. How do role and goal improve prompt effectiveness?
17. Why should prompts include acceptance criteria?
18. How do multimodal inputs improve AI capabilities?
19. What is the relationship between prompt quality and hallucination
    risk?
20. Why isn't a larger AI model always the best choice for every task?

## Scenario-Based Questions (5)

### Scenario 1

A manager wants AI to summarize a PDF into five executive bullet points.
How would you design an effective prompt?

### Scenario 2

A developer asks an AI to debug code but receives inconsistent answers.
How would you improve the prompt?

### Scenario 3

A business wants to automate report generation using AI agents. What
prompt design considerations would you apply?

### Scenario 4

You need an AI to analyze an uploaded graph and explain trends for
executives. How would you structure the prompt?

### Scenario 5

A team is getting generic responses from a multimodal AI. Which prompt
engineering improvements would you recommend?
