## Scenario-Based Questions (5)

### Scenario 1

A retail company wants to summarize millions of customer reviews into
concise product highlights. How would Generative AI help and what
limitations should be considered?

### Scenario 2

A marketing team wants to generate personalized campaigns for millions
of customers. How would you recommend using Generative AI responsibly?

### Scenario 3

A research laboratory wants to accelerate scientific literature reviews
and protein research. How can Generative AI improve the workflow?

### Scenario 4

A manager plans to use AI-generated employee performance feedback. What
review process should be followed before sharing the feedback?

### Scenario 5

A software company wants to choose different AI models for coding,
content generation, and customer support. How would you justify a
multi-LLM strategy?
