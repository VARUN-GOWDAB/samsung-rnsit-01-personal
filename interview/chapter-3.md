# Interview Practice Questions -- Chapter 3: Foundation Models and Platforms

## Conceptual Questions (20)

1.  What is a foundation model, and how does Stanford HAI define it?
2.  Explain the difference between pre-training and fine-tuning in
    foundation models.
3.  Why are foundation models trained on broad datasets before being
    adapted to specific tasks?
4.  What are emergent abilities, and why do they appear in large
    foundation models?
5.  Compare the capabilities of small models and large models.
6.  Differentiate between discriminative AI and generative AI.
7.  Explain the paradigm shift from traditional AI tools to Generative
    AI co-pilots.
8.  What are the limitations of unimodal (text-only) foundation models?
9.  What advantages do multimodal foundation models provide over
    unimodal models?
10. Describe the core capabilities of text and code foundation models.
11. What are the primary capabilities required for AI image generation?
12. Explain the concepts of semantic-to-visual translation and iterative
    refinement in image generation.
13. What are the key capabilities of video and audio foundation models?
14. What is meant by native multimodal architecture?
15. Explain the difference between Transformer-based models and
    Diffusion models.
16. Compare General Purpose AI and Vertical AI in terms of knowledge
    source, strengths, and limitations.
17. Why is domain-specific data important for industry-specific
    foundation models?
18. What are the four common ways to apply foundation models in
    real-world applications?
19. Compare Fine-Tuning and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) as
    approaches for adapting foundation models.
20. How does combining RAG with AI Agents extend the capabilities of
    foundation models beyond question answering?

## Scenario-Based Questions (5)

### Scenario 1

A customer support company wants an AI assistant that can read
screenshots, understand customer emails, and generate responses. Which
type of foundation model would you recommend, and why?

### Scenario 2

A financial organization requires an AI system capable of analyzing
proprietary financial reports with high accuracy and strict compliance
requirements. Would you recommend a General Purpose AI model or a
Vertical AI model? Justify your choice.

### Scenario 3

A software development team needs an AI assistant that can write Python
code, explain algorithms, debug programs, and refactor existing code.
Which task-specific foundation model capabilities are most important for
this application?

### Scenario 4

An enterprise already has a large internal knowledge base and wants an
AI chatbot that always answers using company documents without
retraining the model. Which application approach would you recommend:
Direct API usage, Fine-Tuning, RAG, or RAG + Agent? Explain your
reasoning.

### Scenario 5

A company wants an AI system that can retrieve information from internal
documents, send emails, generate reports, and automatically trigger
business workflows. Which foundation model application strategy best
fits this requirement, and why?
